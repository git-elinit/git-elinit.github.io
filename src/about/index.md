I'm a product designer who likes making things.

I started with iOS development and eventually found my way into product design. But I never really stopped being curious about how things work.

Recently, I've been experimenting with AI agents and building my own software.

Some projects solve everyday problems. Others start because I have an idea and want to see what happens.

This website is where I share what I'm making, what goes wrong, and the things I learn along the way.

Sometimes they're useful. Sometimes they're weird.

And that's part of the fun.
