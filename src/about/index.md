I'm a product designer who likes making things. Sometimes they're useful. Sometimes they're weird.

My background crosses iOS development and product design, which makes me curious about both the way interfaces feel and the systems that make them work.

Lately, that curiosity has turned into a series of independent software experiments. ChatGPT helps me explore ideas and prepare references; Codex handles much of the implementation; and real testing reveals what still needs work.

What interests me most is the space between an idea and a usable product: understanding the problem, deciding what matters, communicating a direction, and noticing the details that don't feel right.

This website is a working record of those experiments. MusicFinder began with a music-library problem. Pantry became a lesson in building and testing an iPhone app with agents. Other projects are still taking shape.

Not everything here is finished. That's intentional. The decisions, mistakes, and changes of direction are part of what I want to share.

Sometimes they're useful. Sometimes they're weird.

And that's part of the fun.
